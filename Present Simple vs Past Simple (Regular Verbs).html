🔥 Warm-up: Present Simple vs Past Simple

⏰ Present Simple
habits, routines, facts
always, usually, every day, on Mondays
⏪ Past Simple
finished actions
yesterday, last week, two days ago
PresentPast
➕I / you / we / they play
he / she / it plays		I / you / he / she / it / we / they played
➖I / you / we / they don’t play
he / she / it doesn’t play		all: didn’t play
❓Do you play?
Does she play?		Did you / she / they play?
Present +s: play → plays · watch → watches · study → studies
Past +ed: walk → walked · like → liked · study → studied
Remember: after don’t, doesn’t, didn’t, Do, Does, Did use the base verb (play, not plays / played).
